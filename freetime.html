Freetime

I'm passionate about ice hockey and team sports, they keep me active, grounded, and offer a great way to connect with people. Whether it's coaching, playing, or cheering, hockey is a lifelong love. I recently also started very active gym to keep up with the physical training and endurance. This helps me to stay focused on other areas of life.

Traveling also plays a big role in my life. I'm always looking for ways to recharge and learn through new cultures, landscapes, and spontaneous adventures. Some people say that traveling is so tiring and they don´t like the airport lounges. I am completely the opposity. I get more energy from the huzzle and buzzle of airport. Expat and nomad by choice and I love it.

I'm also a vintage car enthusiast. Here is my Dodge Dart -64 GT located in Finland. You will often find me during the summer in cruising meet ups around southern Finland. I love the sound of v8 and the smell of petrol.

Family plays a central role in my life. It's the foundation of my support system and a source of daily motivation. Whether it's spending time together outdoors, sharing routines, or simply being present, those are the most meaningful moments. I have two of these tiny monkeys and they are the best, but really... they are the best.
